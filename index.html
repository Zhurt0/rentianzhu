Biography

Hello! I am a PhD Student (2024–2028) in Economics at Copenhagen Business School.

My research centers on two fundamental questions: how do people live, and how can they live better? I investigate wellbeing—the quality of people's current lives—and technology—the processes that drive improvements in living standards. Labor markets are where I examine both: how work affects human flourishing and how technological innovations reshape opportunities for better lives.

I hold an MSc in Advanced Economics and Finance from Copenhagen Business School, and a Bachelor's degree in Global Supply Chain and Logistics Management from The Chinese University of Hong Kong.
